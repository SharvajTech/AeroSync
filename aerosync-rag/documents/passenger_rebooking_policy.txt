TITLE: AeroSync Passenger Rebooking Policy (Demo)
DOCUMENT ID: AS-REBOOK-001

## Purpose

Defines how passengers on a disrupted flight are prioritized for
rebooking, and the thresholds that trigger automatic versus manual
rebooking.

## Rebooking Priority Order

When rebooking passengers from a cancelled or severely delayed flight
(delay exceeding 180 minutes), priority is given in this order:

1. Passengers with a documented medical or accessibility need.
2. Passengers who will miss a connecting international flight.
3. Passengers who will miss a connecting domestic flight.
4. Unaccompanied minors.
5. All other passengers, in order of original booking class (highest
   fare class first), then check-in time.

## Automatic vs. Manual Rebooking

A passenger may be automatically rebooked onto an alternative flight
without agent review if all of the following hold:

- The alternative flight departs within 4 hours of the original
  scheduled departure.
- The alternative flight has available capacity in the same or higher
  booking class.
- The passenger has no special service request on file.

If any of these conditions fail, the passenger should be queued for
manual agent review rather than auto-rebooked.

## Compensation Thresholds

These thresholds govern which passengers are flagged for compensation
review (final compensation decisions are outside the scope of this
policy and this system):

- Delay of 2–4 hours: flagged for meal voucher eligibility.
- Delay over 4 hours or overnight: flagged for meal and accommodation
  eligibility.
- Cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice: flagged for compensation
  review regardless of delay length of the replacement flight.

## Group and Family Handling

Passengers traveling together under a linked booking reference should be
rebooked onto the same alternative flight whenever capacity allows, even
if this means a later departure than the earliest individually available
option.

## Documentation

Every rebooking action must be logged with: passenger reference, original
flight, new flight, priority tier applied, and whether the rebooking was
automatic or manually reviewed.
