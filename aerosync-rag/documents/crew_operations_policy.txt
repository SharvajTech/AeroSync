TITLE: AeroSync Crew Operations Policy (Demo)
DOCUMENT ID: AS-CREW-001

## Purpose

This policy defines how AeroSync's resolution engine and duty officers
should treat crew duty time, rest requirements, and reassignment decisions
when resolving a flight disruption.

## Duty Time Limits

A cockpit or cabin crew member's duty period begins at report time and
ends 30 minutes after the final flight of the day blocks in. Standard duty
limits used by AeroSync's scheduling logic are:

- Maximum single duty period: 13 hours for a two-pilot crew on narrow-body
  aircraft, reduced to 11 hours for duty periods with more than 4 sectors.
- Minimum rest between duty periods: 12 hours, or the length of the
  preceding duty period, whichever is greater.
- A crew member already at or beyond 10 duty hours is flagged internally
  as "high fatigue risk" and should not be assigned an additional sector
  without supervisor override.

## Crew Reassignment Rules

Crew may be reassigned to cover a disrupted flight only if all of the
following hold:

1. The reassignment does not push the crew member's projected duty period
   past the applicable limit in the section above, including the
   estimated duty time of the new sector.
2. The crew member holds a valid type rating for the aircraft in question.
3. At least 45 minutes of turnaround buffer remains between the crew
   member's prior sector and the new report time.
4. If the crew member's remaining duty margin before reassignment is under
   90 minutes, reassignment requires duty-manager sign-off rather than
   automatic approval.

Crew that are already within 60 minutes of their duty limit should not be
reassigned under any circumstances; a fresh crew or standby crew must be
sourced instead, even if this introduces additional delay.

## Standby Crew Activation

Each base maintains a standby crew pool. Standby crew should be activated
in preference to reassigning an already-flying crew member whenever the
projected delay to activate standby is less than the delay that would
result from waiting for the original crew to become legal again.

## Documentation

Every reassignment decision, whether automatic or manager-approved, must
be logged with: crew ID, original assignment, new assignment, duty hours
at time of decision, and the rule under this policy that justified the
decision.
