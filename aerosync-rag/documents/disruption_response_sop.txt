TITLE: AeroSync Disruption Response SOP (Demo)
DOCUMENT ID: AS-SOP-001

## Purpose

Defines the standard sequence of steps AeroSync's resolution engine and
duty officers follow when a disruption is first detected, from detection
through to logged resolution.

## Step 1 — Detection and Classification

On detecting a delay, cancellation, or technical event, the system
classifies its severity:

- Minor: projected delay under 30 minutes, no downstream cascade risk
  flagged.
- Moderate: projected delay 30–120 minutes, or a cascade risk flagged
  against fewer than 3 downstream flights.
- Severe: projected delay over 120 minutes, cancellation, or a cascade
  risk flagged against 3 or more downstream flights.

## Step 2 — Cascade Assessment

For Moderate and Severe events, the resolution engine runs a cascade
assessment across the affected aircraft's remaining rotation and any
crew or gate dependencies, per the cascade simulation logic, before any
resolution options are generated.

## Step 3 — Option Generation

The resolution engine proposes resolution options drawn from the
applicable policies: crew reassignment (see AS-CREW-001), gate
reassignment (see AS-GATE-001), aircraft swap (see AS-SWAP-001), and
passenger rebooking (see AS-REBOOK-001), ranked by estimated total
passenger-minutes of delay avoided.

## Step 4 — Human Review

All Severe-classified events, and any Moderate event where the top-ranked
option requires duty-manager sign-off under its own policy, are queued
for human review before any action is taken. Minor events may be
auto-resolved if the top-ranked option requires no sign-off under any
applicable policy.

## Step 5 — Execution and Logging

Once approved (automatically or by a duty officer), the resolution engine
executes the selected option and writes an audit record containing: the
triggering event, severity classification, all options considered, the
option selected, who approved it (system or named duty officer), and the
outcome.

## Step 6 — Post-Event Review

Severe events are queued for post-event review within 24 hours, to check
whether the cascade assessment's projected impact matched the actual
outcome. Significant mismatches should be flagged for policy or model
review rather than treated as one-off anomalies.

## Escalation

Any event where no resolution option can be generated that avoids
breaching a hard limit in the referenced policies (duty time, minimum
turnaround, swap eligibility) must be escalated to a duty manager
immediately rather than left unresolved in the queue.
